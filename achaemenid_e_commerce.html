Our Heritage

ECHOES OF CYRUS THE GREAT.

Established in 550 BCE, the Achaemenid Empire was the first Persian Empire, stretching across three continents. It was a realm defined by monumental architecture, intricate cuneiform script, and unparalleled cultural synthesis.

We distill that ancient grandeur into modern, minimalist streetwear. The striking geometries of cuneiform and the timeless elegance of the royal falcon are reborn in high-quality, heavy-weight fabrics designed for the urban landscape.

We do not just print clothes; we engrave history.
